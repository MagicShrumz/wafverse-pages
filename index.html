WafVerse

WafVerse is a private Android app for browsing movies and series from the owner's own media services and watching live TV from the owner's own IPTV subscription. It is a personal project and is not offered as a public service.

Sync with Google Drive (optional)

The app can keep Continue Watching, Watchlist and Favorites in step across the owner's own devices. To do this, the owner signs in with their own Google account. The app asks for a single permission, drive.file, which lets it see only the one file it creates itself (wafverse-sync.json) in that account's Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in Drive.

What this app does not do

  • It has no servers of its own and collects no data.
  • It contains no analytics or advertising and shares nothing with third parties.
